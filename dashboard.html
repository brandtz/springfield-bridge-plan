Oregon Education Data Dashboard

Springfield vs. Lane County vs. All 197 Oregon Districts — transparent financial comparison.

🚧

Under Construction

The Oregon Education Data Dashboard will aggregate public ODE financial transparency data across all 197 Oregon school districts. It will show per-pupil spending, enrollment trends, fund balance trajectories, and how Springfield compares to peer districts.

Data sources include ODE Fiscal Transparency Excel files, ODE Fall Membership enrollment reports, and Lane County tax assessment data.
